RoadTrip Pro — Smart Road Trip Planner

A comprehensive, car-focused road-trip planning application designed to unify route navigation, fuel and EV charging station discovery, expense tracking, and vehicle maintenance.

⭐ MAJOR ACADEMIC CAPSTONE PROJECT

RoadTrip Pro

RoadTrip Pro is a car-focused road-trip planning application designed to help users plan journeys, estimate travel and fuel costs, discover places and fuel/EV stations along their route, manage vehicle information, and maintain trip history.

TECHNOLOGY STACK:
HTML5 CSS3 (Responsive) Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) Google Maps JavaScript API Google Places API Firebase Firestore SessionStorage Persistence
View in Project Grid

THE PROBLEM

Planning road trips often requires switching between multiple services for routes, fuel costs, vehicle information, locations, and trip management. Travelers have to calculate fuel expenses manually, search separately for EV chargers or petrol pumps, and juggle disconnected notes for travel budgets and car maintenance.

THE SOLUTION

RoadTrip Pro combines these capabilities into one practical road-trip planning experience. It couples dynamic Google Maps corridor navigation with automated fuel/energy cost estimations, bi-directional map-and-card station discovery, integrated expense budgets, a vehicle maintenance garage, and persistent cloud trip logs.

Key Features & Functional Modules

🗺️
Route Planning

Start and destination selection with interactive Google Maps driving corridor rendering and turn-by-turn route calculations.

⛽
Fuel Cost Estimation

Distance and mileage-based calculations determining fuel volume (Litres) or EV energy (kWh) with accurate journey cost estimates.

⚡
Fuel & EV Stations

Route-corridor discovery for petrol/diesel pumps and EV charging points with ratings, open status, and one-tap Google Maps navigation.

📍
Discover Places

Highway point-of-interest discovery for scenic stopovers, tourist attractions, restaurants, and hotels along the travel corridor.

💰
Trip Budget

Structured financial tracking for fuel, stay, food, and incidentals to keep travel expenses organized before and during the journey.

👥
Group Trips

Collaborative trip planning allowing group travelers to coordinate itineraries, waypoints, and shared road milestones.

📖
Trip Journal

Personal travel logs to capture route notes, memorable road trip highlights, photos, and journal entries for every completed tour.

🚗
Vehicle Management

Dedicated vehicle garage to manage car specs, fuel efficiency, and track periodic service, insurance, and pollution certificate alerts.

🚨
Emergency Features

Direct emergency assistance integration including national emergency helpline (112) and highway assistance helpline (1033).

🔄
Trip History & Persistence

Seamless route persistence across page reloads via SessionStorage and persistent trip archiving with Firebase Cloud Firestore.
